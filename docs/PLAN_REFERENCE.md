# Business plan reference adaptation

The supplied Business Plan Format Guidelines document is a structural reference only. Biz-Z does not include its university identity, course, instructor, student names, submission wording, acknowledgments or sample financial figures.

## Adapted coverage

- Company overview: background, mission, vision and objectives.
- Products and services: features, benefits and distinctive value.
- Market and competition: customers, competitors and SWOT.
- Marketing and sales: product positioning, price, place and promotion.
- Operations and management: ownership, roles, workflow, suppliers and equipment.
- Finance: funding, startup and unit costs, sales, expenses and profit assumptions.
- Executive summary: a concise synthesis written after the main sections.
- Legal compliance: Biz-Z's existing location-specific verification checklist.

The build sequence remains unchanged to preserve saved plans and sequential review gates. PDF reading order puts the executive summary first, followed by company, products, market, marketing, operations, finance and legal compliance. Exports include a business-only cover, contents with actual page references, section page breaks and page numbers. Existing drafts are not silently rewritten; new Zee drafting/revision requests use the adapted guidance.

Optional images, organization diagrams, appendices and formal reference management from the example are not implemented by this adaptation. No citations, logos or financial values are fabricated. Helvetica remains the PDF font; the academic font/format instructions are not mandatory product requirements.
